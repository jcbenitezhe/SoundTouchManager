Privacy notice

SoundTouch Manager, for the Android app on Google Play, the desktop app, and this website. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Who this is

SoundTouch Manager is an unofficial app by Juan Carlos Benitez. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by Bose Corporation. Contact: jcbenitezhe@gmail.com.

There is no SoundTouch Manager account. The app has no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting service.

What stays on your network

The app looks for SoundTouch speakers on your Wi-Fi. Speaker names and local addresses stay on your phone and on the speaker. They are not sent to the developer.

The app does not ask for GPS location. It uses the local network only to find and control speakers.

Music from a DLNA library on your network stays on that network. The app does not upload your files.

Services the app contacts when you use them

  • Internet radio. A station search goes to radio-browser.info. That service sees the search and the network address of the device making it. The audio then plays from the station's own servers, through the speaker.
  • TuneIn. Browsing TuneIn contacts TuneIn. TuneIn is a trademark of TuneIn, Inc. SoundTouch Manager is not affiliated with TuneIn.
  • Spotify. Playback uses your own Spotify account. The login stays on the speaker. Spotify receives a normal Spotify Connect session. The developer does not receive your Spotify password.
  • Station logos. If a station has no logo of its own, the app may ask icons.duckduckgo.com for that station's public site icon. That request contains the station's domain, not your name.

The speaker does not contact the Bose cloud. SoundTouch Manager answers those requests on your own network.

Google Play purchases

On the Android app, optional support purchases are one-time payments through Google Play. Google processes the payment. The developer does not receive your card number. A purchase does not unlock features. The app remains free.

The desktop app's PayPal, Ko-fi, and GitHub Sponsors links open those sites in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy notices. The Android app distributed on Google Play uses Play purchases instead of those links.

Logs you choose to send

The app does not send logs by itself. If you use Save diagnostic logs, it writes a report on your device. That export removes IP addresses and device identifiers. Nothing is uploaded unless you attach the file to an email or a GitHub issue yourself.

This website

jcbenitezhe.github.io/SoundTouchManager sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

SoundTouch Manager is not directed at children. The Google Play listing is for ages 18 and older.

How long data is kept

The developer does not run an account database for this app. Speaker settings live on your speaker and on your device. Purchase records live in your Google account. Website logs, if any, are kept by GitHub.
